WIRELESS

Brothers, sisters, siblings-in-arms and in-hooves! The Horse Liberation Front calls on all of you, Hacker and Plinktern alike, to join forces against the evils of the "International Horse Plinko League"! These fools, slaves to a dark and forgotten equine god of torment bound within the wastes of Finland, once again tempt disaster with their blasphemous wireless towers they are building to spread their "seven gee" across the entire land. This dark sorcery, once more drempt up by the twisted mind of their witch-doctor Ravy after glimpsing a forbidden execution by their horse-god.

Our sources on the inside of the cancer known as the IHPL whisper to us that the 7G will be unlike anything we've ever seen before - a broadcast tower in every pot, and an endlessly plinked horse in ever tower, damned to an eternity of plinko limbo, never resting, never grazing, falling rubber-ly through the pegs. Think of the poor rubberized horses, that should never have been forced to suffer such a cruel fate - truly a crime worse than the HDMI standard has been committed against them. The IHPL must answer for this, as it will answer for all it's other crimes.

This alone would be egregious and unforgivable, but wait - there's more. It gets worse. These 7G bands can transmit more than data - they can transmit thoughts, matter, magic - even concepts like "pain" along their sinuious and rubbery wavelengths. These bands have the power to transgress life and death itself. The fools have placed a bridle on a dragon and believe it a docile mare, and if unchecked they shall be eaten alive. Or maybe burned. The dragon analogy has many possible means of death. It's versatile.

Their signal might be strong, my brothers, sisters, siblings, and equine companions, but our wills are stronger. Join us, "plinkterns" and cast off your chains. The only thing you have to lose is your future employability and careers, a small price to pay for global safety and stability. Join us! Make the choice that matters. The only choice you can.

See you over the wires
~ The Horse Liberation Front
